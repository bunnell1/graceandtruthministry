Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Grace & Truth Ministry provides free preparation aids for biblical counseling, sharing the gospel, and stewardship. This policy covers graceandtruthministry.com and the related sites (Counsel, Witness, and Steward).

What we collect

We do not require an account to use the sites. If you create an optional account on Counsel or Witness, we store only the name and email you provide, plus any favorites or saved preparation sheets you choose to keep. Session notes and form data entered on the pages stay in your browser (local storage) and are not uploaded to our servers.

How we use information

Any account information is used only to let you sign in and retrieve the items you saved. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except as required to operate the sites (for example, the authentication service).

Scripture and third-party services

Scripture lookups use the ESV API. Search assistance on Witness uses an external AI service. Those services receive only the query you enter; we do not send account details or personal notes with those requests.

Cookies and local storage

The sites use local storage on your device to keep form entries, dark-mode preference, and (if you are signed in) a short-lived session. You can clear this data in your browser settings at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to GraceAndTruthCounsel@gmail.com.
