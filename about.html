About Grace & Truth Ministry

Grace & Truth Ministry exists to help ordinary believers live faithfully under Christ. We make simple, Scripture-centered tools that support the local church. Nothing here replaces the Bible, prayer, or pastoral care. These tools are meant to be used alongside them.

Our focus is practical help for three ordinary callings: offering biblical counsel, sharing the gospel, and stewarding what God has given.

Biblical Counseling

counsel.graceandtruthministry.com

A preparation aid for biblical counseling. It helps you gather relevant Scripture, biblical principles, and practical tools before a session. You can search common struggles, open growth modules, and use fillable forms that stay on your device. It is designed to support pastors and lay counselors who want to keep Christ and Scripture at the center of care.

Sharing the Gospel

witness.graceandtruthministry.com

Help for sharing the gospel in ordinary conversations. You can look up common objections and situations, review biblical principles and sample response directions, and prepare with simple tools. It is meant for believers who want to speak about Christ with clarity and kindness when the opportunity comes.

Stewardship

steward.graceandtruthministry.com

Tools for biblical stewardship of money, family, and daily life. It helps you think about what God has entrusted to you and how to manage it under Christ. The focus is practical faithfulness in ordinary responsibilities.

How we approach this

We hold to the authority of Scripture and the sufficiency of Christ. The tools aim to be clear, useful, and free of unnecessary complexity. Data that you enter stays on your device unless you choose to create an account for features that sync across devices.

These resources are offered freely. We hope they serve the church and help believers speak and live with grace and truth.
